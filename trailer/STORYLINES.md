# Storylines: tráiler de Chatarra Z (2–3 min)

## A. De piedras a fuego (ELEGIDA)
Device: life before and after, the progression itself is the story. Form: one home stage (the wall) that the film keeps returning to, with chapter cards that burst out of the thing just thrown.
The viewer starts with one survivor and a handful of stones against a horde, learns the drag-and-release throw, then watches the arsenal grow level by level (sticks, sling, bow, fire arrows, molotov) under the constant pressure of limited ammo, until the Mamut falls in fire.
- First 2 s: a stone already in the air, it hits a zombie in the head, the head flies off, "¡A LA CABEZA!" pops in-game.
- Beats: hook → the horde → title → how to throw → 8 level chapters → every arrow counts (materials + crafting) → upgrades → boss → claim → end card.
- Look: the game's own world (sunset junkyard), hazard yellow #ffc229 and rust #c8392b on soot #1a140e, Archivo Black titles.
- Sound: the game's own synthesized soundtrack as the bed, bundled whoosh/impact on cuts, riser into the boss kill.

## B. Una noche en la muralla
Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: one continuous take of a single long night, the camera never cuts, it pushes into the HUD to explain each element as it matters. Calmer, survival-diary tone, lines like hour stamps ("21:04 · se acaban las flechas").

## C. El taller
Device: the object is the explanation. Form: object-to-object chain. Everything starts on the workbench: wood, cloth and scrap become arrows, a molotov, a barricade; each crafted item is carried straight into the battle shot that uses it, then the loot flies back to the bench.
